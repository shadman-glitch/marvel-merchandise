Frequently Asked Questions
How long does express delivery take in India? +
All orders dispatch within 24 hours directly from our central fulfillment facility via Bluedart Air Express. Metro deliveries arrive within 24-48 hours, while other locations take 2-4 business days. All shipments are fully insured against transit damage.
Are these official movie-accurate 1:1 scale replicas? +
Yes, every collectible is 100% officially licensed Marvel Verse merchandise. Wearable helmets are engineered with 1:1 life-size proportions, motorized servo opening, voice control microcontrollers, and authentic metallic electroplated finishes.
What is the return and replacement policy? +
We provide a 7-day replacement guarantee if any item arrives defective, damaged in transit, or missing accessories. Each piece includes a 1-Year Stark Care Warranty covering electronic components, audio speakers, and motor servos.
What payment methods are supported? +
We support UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), all major Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay), Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery (COD) with verification.
How do wearable helmets fit? +
Helmets are molded to fit adult head circumferences up to 62 cm comfortably. Inside, they feature high-density memory foam padding and ergonomic adjustable straps for a secure and comfortable fit.
